Paper size and alignment

The full certificate and the duplicate keep the left margin free for the punch holes and are centred in the rest of the page. “Details only” ignores the margins: print one test page, hold it against the booklet page to a light, and adjust the two move values until the lines match. In the browser print window, choose the same paper size, margins “None” and scale 100%.

Every field on the certificate can be edited before printing. Changes apply to the printed certificate and its duplicate and do not change the Excel file.
